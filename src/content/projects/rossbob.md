<section class="summary">
  <p>Info: A 24h hackathon project I worked on with Dominic Peel, Martin Lombard, and Kobi Kelemen.</p>
  <p>Problem: A common criticism of image-gen software is that it isn't "real" like human-made art.</p>
  <p>Solution: A software worksflow that transforms images from Dalle-3 into brush-stroke instructions for human-like painting by a 6DoF robot.</p>
